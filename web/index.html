01 / FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE

Every dollar has a definition.

Cost initiatives count avoidable operating expense. Revenue initiatives count incremental revenue multiplied by contribution margin. Capacity released counts only after an expense reduction is evidenced.

Incremental EBITDA = probability-weighted gross benefit × realization × ramp − recurring opex − implementation expense

Capex reduces cash, not EBITDA. Receivables acceleration releases cash, not new revenue. All upfront investment occurs in month 1; operating costs begin at launch, including failed initiatives.

Run rate is a steady-state annualized expectation, not this year's result. Payback is the first month of nonnegative cumulative modeled cash and may be followed by later outflows.

02 / EXACT OPTIMIZATION

A defensible portfolio.

The engine enumerates every subset of the candidate set and maximizes expected 12-month incremental EBITDA. It enforces budget, delivery capacity, prerequisites and mutually exclusive benefit pools.

A shared service labor pool prevents claiming both automation and scheduling savings. A revenue pool prevents counting overlapping leakage and pricing opportunities twice.

Scope is deliberately bounded to 20 initiatives. Results are exact for the inputs, not a guarantee that the assumptions are correct. Dependencies represent delivery prerequisites, not conditional success probabilities.

03 / VALUE ASSURANCE

Forecasts become evidence.

Recognized impact = observed benefit − counterfactual benefit − incremental operating costs − implementation expense. The ledger rejects duplicate period / benefit-pool combinations.

Approval requires an evidence reference, reviewer and approval date. This demo validates these fields; it does not authenticate reviewers or verify source documents. Production use needs controlled workflows, immutable storage and finance sign-off.

04 / HONEST BOUNDARIES

Engineered to be inspectable.

All figures and organizations are synthetic. No employer data, customer records or API keys are used. Deterministic calculations drive every recommendation; there is no LLM inventing financial results.

EBITDA is a non-GAAP measure. This workbench models incremental impacts and does not provide a GAAP reconciliation or financial reporting system. The model excludes tax, financing, terminal value and detailed cash timing.
